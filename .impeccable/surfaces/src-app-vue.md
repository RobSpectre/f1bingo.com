# Surface: the bingo board during the 2026 São Paulo GP (Senna tribute)

Scope: the whole app (header, board, winner card, footer, share capture) while the tribute window is open (5–10 Nov 2026). Visitor mode: Operate. The task is unchanged: tap squares as things happen in the race, win a line, share the card.

Audience: F1 fans watching the São Paulo GP, mostly glancing at a phone. Constraint: time-boxed and removable; no likeness, photos or official marks.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Ayrton's home city holds his card. The board hangs under a green span, like MASP floats over its open plaza like the museum floats over its open plaza. This refuses the category default of a near-black dashboard with neon glows.
OWN-WORLD: Warm raw-concrete ground, flag green structure (header beam, pillars), flag blue for the free square and primary button, clear glass panels for squares on small concrete plinths, helmet yellow for marked squares, near-black ink. Tomorrow (made by São Paulo designers) for display, Source Sans 3 for body.
STORY: The fan sees a card that is clearly about Ayrton and São Paulo, plays exactly as before, and shares a card that looks like nothing else.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A green beam across the top carries "Formula 1 Bingo", the event and the actions. Under it sits a dedication line, "Obrigado, Ayrton". The 5×5 glass-easel grid fills the rest. Share and reset stay top-right on desktop and full-width on phones.
FORM: candidate 6 of 7 (MASP / São Paulo modernism, Lina Bo Bardi's red span and crystal easels), seed:fd96b8b2e52dbd61 (degraded roll, no challengers).
SIGNATURE: Squares are glass easels. Marking one fills the pane with helmet yellow from the plinth up.
